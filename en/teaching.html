Teaching

The ROGLE group teaches business management at university level. To assist people who need to deliver Operations Management education online, the group organizes the videos it has recorded over the past years around topics.

Open teaching materials

Institutional repository ROGLE. Teaching materials The group's collection in RiUNET, the repository of the UPV: cases, texts and course materials, with their licence. Opens riunet.upv.es, the repository of the UPV.

Teaching videos

Video repository Teaching videos on operations management A spreadsheet with some of the group's videos, compiled and classified by topic. Opens docs.google.com, a Google service that may use cookies.

This website embeds no videos or third-party content. That is why the resource is offered as a link.
