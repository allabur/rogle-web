Research

ROGLE does research on operations management, production, logistics and work organization. This page gathers the group’s research lines, projects and articles. Each article has a record with its APA citation and BibTeX.

Research lines

  • Application and adaptation of Organization Engineering techniques in work environments involving people with disabilities.
  • University teaching of business management.
  • Balancing assembly lines considering realistic factors.
  • Evaluation and improvement of productive and logistical systems (lean production, reengineering).
  • Human resources management in new production environments.
  • Efficiency and sustainability indicators in industrial companies.
  • Production scheduling and sequencing.
  • Social and environmental industrial sustainability.

A research line of special interest is the application of industrial engineering and operations management techniques to increase work accessibility for people with disabilities. The range of activities goes from software design for production planning and scheduling to the development of in-company courses.
