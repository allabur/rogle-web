Members

These are the people of the ROGLE group, from the Departamento de Organización de Empresas of the Universitat Politècnica de València. Each card links only to the profiles that the person has made public.

Group leader (1)

Pilar I. Vidal-Carreras

Associate professor of operations management

Departamento de Organización de Empresas

Research interests: Operations Management and Lean Manufacturing

  • UPV profile
Full profile

Pilar I. Vidal-Carreras, Ph.D. is an associate professor of operations management in the Departamento de Organización de Empresas at the Universidad Politécnica de Valencia. She obtained her Industrial Engineering degree and her Ph.D. from the Universidad Politécnica de Valencia (Spain). Her research and teaching interests are focused on Operations Management and Lean Manufacturing in various sectors, such as industrial and healthcare. She has published numerous articles in prestigious conferences and journals and has participated in research projects at the European, university, regional, and Spanish Ministry levels. Currently, she is one of the principal investigators of a project developing resource planning models for individuals with diverse abilities.

Also noteworthy is her research in education. Since a significant portion of her activity is devoted to teaching, the professor considers it essential not only to conduct research to ensure the highest possible knowledge in the subjects she teaches but also to investigate how to effectively convey that knowledge to students. Therefore, she has also published in the area of quality in teaching and has participated in 11 teaching innovation projects, serving as the principal investigator in two of them. It can be said that Pilar I. Vidal-Carreras’s curriculum is intertwined with one objective: to promote effective convergence between research and the needs of society, whether in the industrial, service, or educational fields, with a particular focus on the transfer and practical application of knowledge.

Carlos Andrés Romano

University Full Professor

Departamento de Organización de Empresas

Research interests: Cellular Manufacturing, Work Study, Production Scheduling and Sequencing, Combinatorial Optimization

  • UPV profile
  • LinkedIn
Full profile

Carlos Andrés Romano, has been a Doctor of Industrial Engineering since 2001 and a University Full Professor since 2017 in the Departamento de Organización de Empresas of the Polytechnic University of Valencia. He teaches at the Higher Technical School of Industrial Engineers of Valencia.

His research career has focused on more than 25 years of activity in areas such as Cellular Manufacturing, Work Study, Production Scheduling and Sequencing or Combinatorial Optimization mainly. Having developed in these areas Methodologies, Conceptual Frameworks, Operational Research Models (OR/MS) and Artificial Intelligence (AI), Decision Support Systems (DSS) and Digital Technologies, in a deterministic and uncertain context. The main industrial sectors in which he has worked are ceramics, automobiles, footwear and food distribution.

His research has taken the form of 3 six-year periods of research and 1 of transfer, 9 doctoral theses in the last 10 years, more than 400 total citations of his 30 articles in indexed journals of which 47% are in the first tertile and 37 % in the second tertile, 12 book chapters in prestigious international publishers and participation in nearly 140 conferences, almost all of them international peer review, in addition to participation in 38 research projects funded in public and private calls.

In the teaching field, he has participated in 28 teaching innovation projects, where it is worth highlighting the award for teaching excellence that he received in 2001 and the 2nd award received at the UPV for the application of Flip Teaching methodologies to the GIOi subject of “Programming and Production and Operations Control”.

He has been or is a member of the following scientific associations: “Royal Economic Society of Friends of the Country of Valencia”, “EURO -The Association of European Operational Research Societies”, “Association for the Development of Organization Engineering – ADINGOR” and “ Spanish Society of Statistics and Operational Research SEIO.

Lourdes Canós Darós, Ph.D. is Full Professor in the Business Management Department at the Universidad Politécnica de Valencia. José P. Garcia-Sabater

Full Professor of Operations Management

Departamento de Organización de Empresas

Research interests: supply chain management, operations management, management science

  • Personal website
Full profile

José P. Garcia-Sabater is Full Professor of Operations Management in the Departamento de Organización de Empresas at the Universidad Politécnica de Valencia. He received his Ingeniero Industrial Degree and his PhD from the University Politécnica de Valencia (Spain). He also received a Combined Eng Degree from Coventry University (UK). Professor Garcia-Sabater’s research and teaching interests are in the areas of supply chain management, operations management, and management science. He is member of the Scientific Committee of congresses and Editorial Boards. His work has appeared in journals such as European Journal of Operational Research, International Journal of Production Economics, Fuzzy Sets and Systems, Discrete Applied Mathematics, and others.

Prof. Garcia-Sabater’s research has focused on empirical studies of operations management in several key industries mainly at the Automotive Industry. These studies have examined mainly Production Planning and Inventory Coordination, but he has also developed in other areas like Assembly Line Balancing for handicapped people. His algorithms are running the assembly line sequencing process at Ford España Assembly Line, and many other SME enterprises are using software and algorithms that has been developed by himself. He also leads the Research Team ROGLE focused on the links Operations Management.

Prof Garcia-Sabater actively leads projects with industrial companies mainly ate the automotive sector and the food industry. He has led (and leads) a number of regional and national funded projects, and has partipated (and participates) in European Funded Projects.

Julio Juan García-Sabater, Ph.D. is an associate professor in the Departamento de Organización de Empresas at the Polytechnic University of Valencia. Ph. D. Juan A. Marin-Garcia is a Professor, member of the Departamento de Organización de Empresas at the Universitat Politècnica de València (Spain). Cristobal Miralles Insa

Full Professor

Departamento de Organización de Empresas

Research interests: design of systems and workplaces accessible for people with disabilities through the use of Industrial Engineering tools

  • ResearchGate
Full profile

Cristóbal Miralles is PhD. In Industrial Engineering and Full Professor at the Industrial Engineering Faculty of Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV) in Spain, where he also lectures at the Master on Corporate Social Responsibility. He is member of the Research group ROGLE at UPV Management Science Dept., with post-docs at Cardiff Business School (2004), University of Leeds (2010), Universidade de São Paulo (2012) and Université Grenoble-Alpes (2019) and short stages in many other.

His main research line is focused on the design of systems and workplaces accessible for people with disabilities through the use of Industrial Engineering tools; with multiple works published at international conferences and indexed journals, and also dealing with evaluation/edition tasks for some of them. From his expertise in applied projects and consultancy, his research approach is also widen to other stakeholders within the CSR framework, defining models for translating sustainable policies & strategies into the best operative practices.

He lectures in four languages and is usual invited professor at prestige universities both European (e.g. Liverpool John Moores, Université Grenoble-Alpes…) and Latin-American (e.g. Universidade de São Paulo, Los Andes in Bogotá…), and during five years (2011-2015) has been the Chair and coordinator of the Working Group on “Ethics and OR”, a research network with more than 80 members inside EURO (European Association of Operational Research). Since then, he is in charge of CEDAT Foundation within the UPV Vice-Chancellor of Art, Science, Technology and Society of UPV. This foundation deals with different issues related with Disability, contributing to minimize the accessibility barriers of students both in the Campus and in the labor market. Through its Sheltered Work Centre, the foundation also promotes the socio-labor integration in collaboration with local stakeholders and launching visible activities that increase awareness in (and out) the UPV community.

Since 2022, he is the director of the Valencian Interuniversity Chair for Innovation in Social Services, working closely with companies, institutions, and social agents in the region, and supporting several Service-Learning collaborations. These experiences, together with his inner interest on Values Education, have resulted in several case studies and moral dilemmas that he applies at the classroom in order to build critical&ethical reasoning skills in the students.
