Legal notice and privacy

Draft pending review. This text is a proposal from the group. The UPV legal service must review it before it is final. The Spanish version is the reference text.

Who publishes this website

This website belongs to the ROGLE Research Group of the Department of Business Organization of the Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV). Address: Building 7D (Edificio 7D), Camino de Vera, s/n, 46022 València, Spain.

The website is hosted on the UPV advanced web service, at rogle.webs.upv.es. The UPV owns the service and the domain.

What data we publish

  • About group members: full name, position or role, department and links to public profiles (UPV directory, ORCID, Scopus, Google Scholar, ResearchGate, LinkedIn and personal websites). These data were already public on the group’s previous website.
  • About projects and articles: the names of participants and authors, as they appear in the public authorship information.
  • We do not publish email addresses, phone numbers, photographs or other private data.

Cookies and tracking

This website is static. It uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no forms. It loads no fonts, scripts or images from other domains. The table filter on the Research page runs in your browser and sends nothing.

Like any web server, the UPV hosting service may log technical connection data, such as the IP address and the date of the visit, for security and operation. The UPV manages that log.

Links to other sites

Some links lead to third-party services, such as Google Sheets, ORCID or LinkedIn. Those sites have their own cookies and privacy policies. The group does not control them.

Your rights

If you appear on this website and want to access, correct or remove your data, tell the person who leads the group. Their profile is on the Members page. You can also write to the group’s postal address (see Contact).

Under the General Data Protection Regulation and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency.

Intellectual property and trademarks

Unless stated otherwise, the texts and data belong to the ROGLE Group or to their authors. The ROGLE name and logo belong to the group. The UPV logo belongs to the UPV and may not be modified. Linking to this website is free.

Liability

The group tries to keep the information up to date. The project and article data come from the previous website and were last updated on 26 March 2024. If you find an error, tell the person who leads the group and we will correct it.
