Resources

What the group makes available to anyone who needs it: its visual identity, document and slide templates, the publication list as BibTeX and the teaching videos.

  • Brand and visual identity

    Logos, palette, typefaces, usage rules, Word, PowerPoint, LaTeX and Quarto templates, print kit and presentation videos. All downloadable.

  • Publications as BibTeX

    The group’s journal articles in a single .bib file, for your reference manager. Each article also has a record on Research.

  • Teaching repository

    Operations management videos, compiled and classified by topic.

  • Software and code

    Under construction. The programs, models and data that the group publishes will go here.
