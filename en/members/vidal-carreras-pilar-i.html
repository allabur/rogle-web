Pilar I. Vidal-Carreras, Ph.D. is an associate professor of operations management in the Departamento de Organización de Empresas at the Universidad Politécnica de Valencia. She obtained her Industrial Engineering degree and her Ph.D. from the Universidad Politécnica de Valencia (Spain). Her research and teaching interests are focused on Operations Management and Lean Manufacturing in various sectors, such as industrial and healthcare. She has published numerous articles in prestigious conferences and journals and has participated in research projects at the European, university, regional, and Spanish Ministry levels. Currently, she is one of the principal investigators of a project developing resource planning models for individuals with diverse abilities.

Also noteworthy is her research in education. Since a significant portion of her activity is devoted to teaching, the professor considers it essential not only to conduct research to ensure the highest possible knowledge in the subjects she teaches but also to investigate how to effectively convey that knowledge to students. Therefore, she has also published in the area of quality in teaching and has participated in 11 teaching innovation projects, serving as the principal investigator in two of them. It can be said that Pilar I. Vidal-Carreras’s curriculum is intertwined with one objective: to promote effective convergence between research and the needs of society, whether in the industrial, service, or educational fields, with a particular focus on the transfer and practical application of knowledge.
