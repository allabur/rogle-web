Juan Martínez Tomás is a lecturer in the Departamento de Organización de Empresas at the Universitat Politècnica de València and holds a PhD in Business Administration. He has more than twenty years of industry experience as a production and operations manager.

His research focuses on participative management, continuous improvement and high-involvement work practices, with special attention to the AMO model (ability, motivation and opportunity) and its impact on people and organisations. He also works on the competencies of operations managers, on search filters for systematic reviews and on the use of artificial intelligence in research.

His career combines industrial experience in international and project-based settings with academic research applied to business.
