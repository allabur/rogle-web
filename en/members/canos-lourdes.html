Lourdes Canós Darós, Ph.D. is Full Professor in the Business Management Department at the Universidad Politécnica de Valencia. She has a degree in Business and Administration and her Ph.D. is about human resources management. She is a member of the research group ROGLE. She has held the position of Deputy Director of Tourism Management in Gandia Campus at the Universitat Politècnica de València and the Deputy Head of Department for Secretary, and today she is part of different management committes. She has published papers in different international journals with recognized quality in the management field. As recognition, she has received awards for her research and for her work on teaching innovation. Moreover, she is an active participant in different meetings around the world together with other lecturers and managers of important companies. She has taught in different courses and seminars around the world. These conferences were about human resources management, management skills, decision making and management under uncertainty, touristic companies, skills and high education teaching.
