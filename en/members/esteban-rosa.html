Rosa Esteban-Amaro is a Professor in the Departamento de Organización de Empresas at the Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV), Spain. She holds a PhD in Business Administration and has over 20 years of professional experience in industry.

Her research interests include Operations Management, Artificial Intelligence and digital transformation, circular economy, sustainable operations and eco-innovation. Her research examines organisational and operational transformation across firms and value chains, with particular attention to SMEs, organisational learning and capability development.

She collaborates with international research institutions and industry partners, combining academic research with real-world challenges related to digitalisation, innovation and sustainability.
