Julien Maheut, PhD, is an Associate Professor (Titular de Universidad) in the Departamento de Organización de Empresas (Departamento de Organización de Empresas) at the Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV), Spain. He holds a degree in Industrial Engineering from the UPV and in Diplôme d'Ingénieur from the École Nationale Supérieure d'Arts et Métiers (ENSAM, France), both earned in 2008, the latter with an Excellence Award, and a PhD from the UPV (2013).

His work spans operations research, operations and supply chain management, and the design of optimisation and simulation models. Recent research focuses on workforce planning and assignment, skill-based workforce design, stochastic optimisation, LLM and metaheuristics. He has published in journals such as Production Planning & Control, Flexible Services and Manufacturing Journal, European Journal of Industrial Engineering, Central European Journal of Operations Research, Mathematics, Sustainability, Results In Engineering, Systems, Cuadernos de Gestión, etc.

His teaching, research and knowledge-transfer work feed into each other. He teaches operations management, quantitative methods in Operations Management, Logistics & Supply Chain Processes and supervises Master's theses in the Master's in Industrial Engineering (MUII). He has led consulting and R&D projects to design, implement and improve operations management systems for companies including Ford Motor Company, Faurecia (Forvia), Johnson Controls, Schneider Electric and NTT Data.
