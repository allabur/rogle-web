Julien Maheut

Position
Associate Professor
Category
Teaching and research staff
  • UPV profile
JM

Industrial Engineer from the UPV in 2008, Mechanical Engineer from the ENSAM (France) in 2008 with Excellence Award and Doctor Industrial Engineer by the UPV.

Currently an Associate Professor at the UPV, he teaches courses such as Operations Management I & II, Lean Manufacturing, Quantitative Methods for IO, Work Study, Artificial Intelligence, Logistics & Operations Consulting, etc.

His professional experience is related to the design, implementation and improvement of operations management systems, having worked on projects for companies such as Ford Motor Company, Faurecia, Johnson Controls, Schneider Electric, NTT Data, etc.

Authors of more than 10 papers in JCR journals, more than 50 publications in other journals and prestigious books. I have participated in 5 European projects, 4 national research projects and more than 15 research contracts with companies.
