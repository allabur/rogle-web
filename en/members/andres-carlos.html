Carlos Andrés Romano, has been a Doctor of Industrial Engineering since 2001 and a University Full Professor since 2017 in the Departamento de Organización de Empresas of the Polytechnic University of Valencia. He teaches at the Higher Technical School of Industrial Engineers of Valencia.

His research career has focused on more than 25 years of activity in areas such as Cellular Manufacturing, Work Study, Production Scheduling and Sequencing or Combinatorial Optimization mainly. Having developed in these areas Methodologies, Conceptual Frameworks, Operational Research Models (OR/MS) and Artificial Intelligence (AI), Decision Support Systems (DSS) and Digital Technologies, in a deterministic and uncertain context. The main industrial sectors in which he has worked are ceramics, automobiles, footwear and food distribution.

His research has taken the form of 3 six-year periods of research and 1 of transfer, 9 doctoral theses in the last 10 years, more than 400 total citations of his 30 articles in indexed journals of which 47% are in the first tertile and 37 % in the second tertile, 12 book chapters in prestigious international publishers and participation in nearly 140 conferences, almost all of them international peer review, in addition to participation in 38 research projects funded in public and private calls.

In the teaching field, he has participated in 28 teaching innovation projects, where it is worth highlighting the award for teaching excellence that he received in 2001 and the 2nd award received at the UPV for the application of Flip Teaching methodologies to the GIOi subject of “Programming and Production and Operations Control”.

He has been or is a member of the following scientific associations: “Royal Economic Society of Friends of the Country of Valencia”, “EURO -The Association of European Operational Research Societies”, “Association for the Development of Organization Engineering – ADINGOR” and “ Spanish Society of Statistics and Operational Research SEIO.
